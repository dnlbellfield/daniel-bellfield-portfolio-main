About me

I connect the idea to the finished campaign.

I’m Daniel, a web and email developer who enjoys making complicated digital work feel manageable. I help teams turn designs and campaign plans into emails, landing pages, forms, and other pieces that are ready to use.

I’m often the person connecting creative ideas with the technical details. I ask the practical questions, solve production problems, check that everything works, and keep the handoff clear for everyone involved.

✦

In plain English: I make sure the digital pieces look right, work properly, and are ready when a campaign needs to go live.
