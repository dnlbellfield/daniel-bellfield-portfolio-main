About me

A little about me.

I’m a web and email developer who’s been working in digital production since 2022. Most of my professional experience comes from agency work, where I’ve helped develop and launch email campaigns, landing pages, donation forms, and other digital projects.

I enjoy the mix of creative and technical work—taking a design or idea, figuring out how to make it function, and working through the details until everything comes together. I’ve worked across front-end development, email production, marketing platforms, and troubleshooting, often collaborating with designers and campaign teams along the way. Outside of my day-to-day work, I build independent projects to explore ideas, learn new technologies, and solve problems that interest me.
