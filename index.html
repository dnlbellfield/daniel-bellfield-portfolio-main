About me

About my work.

I’m a web and email developer focused on campaign production. I turn plans and designs into emails, pages, forms, and other pieces teams can use. I also troubleshoot issues and help keep creative, technical requirements, and timelines aligned.

Since 2022, I’ve worked on responsive HTML emails, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, website updates, donation pages, testing, and small workflow tools. Outside of client work, I build projects to try ideas and keep learning.

How I work

How I support digital campaigns

Emails, web pages, and forms often work together as part of a campaign. I help build, set up, and test those pieces so they’re ready to use.
